SWAPPY POP!

A windowless van approaches you offering candy. And just like you did as a kid, you accepted.

Except this time they actually do have candy for you. It's a Swappy Pop!

What it does: Swappy Pop lets you swap any two active letters on the cube. Not used tiles. Not blank ghost tiles. Active letters. We have standards, which is disturbing because we also have van candy.

How to use it: Press I to open Inventory, click Swappy Pop, then click two active letters on the cube. The letters trade places, one Swappy Pop is consumed, and the cube files another complaint with Human Resources.

Use it when one cursed letter is blocking a good word, a contract, or your fragile belief that this economy is fair.
